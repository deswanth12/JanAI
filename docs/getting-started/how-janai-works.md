# 🧭 How JanAI Works — A Citizen Journey

This page explains how **JanAI** empowers citizens and government officers, written in plain language for program managers, government partners, and stakeholders.

---

## 🌟 The 6-Step Citizen Journey

```
 1. Search Scheme ➔ 2. AI Reasoning ➔ 3. Verify Criteria ➔ 4. Encrypted Vault ➔ 5. Submit Application ➔ 6. Instant Alerts
```

### 1. 🔍 Citizen Searches for a Scheme
A citizen visits JanAI and types or speaks their query in English or their native regional language (e.g., *"Scholarship for B.Tech"* or *"Rythu Bharosa farmer benefit"*). Citizens do not need to memorize complex government department acronyms.

### 2. ⚡ JanAI Finds Relevant Gazette Data
Within 20 milliseconds, JanAI searches across 420+ central and state welfare scheme databases, returning verified government gazette information.

### 3. 🤖 Citizen Checks AI Eligibility
The citizen asks the JanAI Copilot to evaluate their household eligibility. JanAI evaluates household income, age, occupation, and land ownership against official gazette rules with zero hallucinations and transparent citations.

### 4. 🔒 Documents Are Securely Uploaded
The citizen uploads required proof documents (Aadhaar, Income Certificate). JanAI inspects the file's raw binary signature (Magic Bytes) to ensure authenticity before storing it encrypted in the AES-256 vault.

### 5. 📄 Application Is Submitted
The application is assigned a unique tracking ID and routed to the relevant nodal officer or partner organization (e.g., Andhra University CSC).

### 6. 📱 Multi-Channel Status Updates Are Provided
As the application progresses through verification milestones, the citizen receives instant status notifications via SMS, Email, Push, and In-App alerts.
