# 👤 Citizen User Guide

Welcome to JanAI! This guide helps citizens discover welfare benefits, evaluate eligibility using AI, and upload document vaults safely.

---

## 🚀 Quick Steps
1. **Search Schemes**: Enter your query in English or your native language (e.g. *Post-Matric Scholarship*, *Pattadar Passbook*).
2. **Ask AI Copilot**: Speak or type to evaluate household eligibility.
3. **Upload Vault**: Store Aadhaar, Income, and Caste certificates encrypted in your private vault.
